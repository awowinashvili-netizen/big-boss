Put the car photos in this folder.

Then reference them from src/data/cars.ts (see the project README).
Files here are served at /cars/<filename> — e.g. this note is at /cars/README.txt.

Suggested naming:
  <car-id>-side.jpg        the side-profile shot (the one that animates)
  <car-id>-1.jpg .. -4.jpg the gallery shots

Suggested size: ~2000px on the long edge, JP/WebP, compressed. The layout
reserves the right aspect ratio, so a missing or slow photo never shifts it.
